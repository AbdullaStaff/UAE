Pillars of Emirati Culture

☕

Al Majlis & Hospitality

Generosity (Al Karam) and family gatherings define Emirati life. Guests are welcomed in the Majlis with aromatic Gahwa (Arabic coffee with cardamom and saffron) and dates.

🦅

Falconry & Desert Heritage

A UNESCO-recognized heritage passed down through generations, symbolizing courage, patience, and deep respect for the Arabian desert.

🥁

Al Ayala Traditional Dance

Performed at Eid Al Etihad celebrations across all seven emirates, rows of performers move in harmony with bamboo canes to traditional drums and chanted poetry.

⛵

Pearl Diving & Maritime Roots

Wooden Dhow boats and pearl diving shaped coastal communities, building resilience, teamwork, and brotherhood long before 1971.

🧵

Al Sadu Weaving & Attire

Traditional Al Sadu weaving showcases geometric desert patterns, while citizens celebrate National Day in the crisp white Kandura and embroidered Mukhawar.

🍯

Emirati Cuisine

Families gather over traditional dishes including Machboos, Harees, Thareed, and golden Luqaimat topped with date syrup and sesame.

55 Years of the Union

18 February 1968

The Historic Meeting at Al Samha

Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan and Sheikh Rashid bin Saeed Al Maktoum met to lay the foundation for a unified federation.

2 December 1971

Birth of the United Arab Emirates

At Union House in Dubai, the Rulers signed the declaration establishing the UAE and raised the national flag for the first time.

10 February 1972

Ras Al Khaimah Joins the Union

Ras Al Khaimah officially joined the federation, completing the seven emirates united under one flag.
